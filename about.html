My name is Úna Roberts and I am a senior at Columbia College Chicago. I am pursuing a degree in animation with a minor in programming, am enrolled in the Combined Degree program and have started my master's in Arts Management. I have been doing art since I was very young, and have experience with drawing, traditional and digital, painting, block prints, sculpture and collage. I have now focused in on computer animation as my main medium. I love telling stories through art and giving people a place to escape to. I have experience in Autodesk Maya, Nuke, Toon Boom Harmony, Storyboard Pro and the Adobe Suite.
